THE GAMEPLAY IS THE LEARNING.

01

Experience first

The phenomenon appears before the technical vocabulary.

02

Failure gives information

A failure shows what changed, what still works, and where to look.

03

Repetition is hidden

The same concept returns in different situations instead of appearing as a separate “review” exercise.

One persistent build

The same robot evolves throughout the campaign. A late change can break something that worked earlier.
