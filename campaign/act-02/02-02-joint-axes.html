NAME WHAT YOU OBSERVED

AXIS + ANGLE = JOINT MOTION.

A revolute joint constrains the child link to rotate around a defined axis. For a 1-DoF joint, the independent joint coordinate q is the rotation angle around that axis.

JOINT AXIS = direction of allowed rotation in a named frame
q = amount of rotation around that axis
1 DoF = one independent coordinate
